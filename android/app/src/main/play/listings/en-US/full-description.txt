Bazarr is an unofficial companion app for people who run their own Bazarr server. Browse your series and movies, see which subtitles are missing, search and download subtitles, follow the history and keep an eye on the job queue, all from a clean, modern mobile interface.

NO ACCOUNT. NO DATABASE.
There is no sign-up and no login. On first launch you enter the address of your server and your Bazarr API key. That's it. Your settings stay on your device.

TRY IT WITHOUT A SERVER
Don't have Bazarr yet? Tap "Try demo" on the first screen to explore every feature with built-in sample data. No server, account or credentials needed.

RESILIENT CONNECTION
Set a primary and a secondary URL (for example your home network address and your public domain). If the primary one can't be reached, the app switches to the secondary one automatically.

FEATURES
• Series: library list with search, filters and subtitle progress per series
• Series details: episodes, existing and missing subtitles, scan disk, search missing subtitles and sync with Sonarr
• Episode details: subtitle files, per-episode history and manual subtitle search across your providers
• Movies: the same experience for your Radarr movies
• History: every subtitle that was downloaded, upgraded, synced or deleted, for series and movies
• Jobs: running, pending, failed and completed jobs plus scheduled tasks you can run on demand
• More: server status, health checks, providers status and wanted counters
• Fast: your library is cached locally and opens instantly, then refreshes in the background
• Material 3 design with a dark theme

REQUIREMENTS
You need a running Bazarr instance that your phone can reach and its API key, found under Settings > General > Security in Bazarr.
